WHY I MADE THIS
The QR code was supposed to be the easy part.
I’m Sevgi Gokce Kafali, a researcher. When I needed QR codes for posters, I kept running into ads, account walls, and charges for simple things. So I made the tool I wanted to use myself.
You can generate and download QR codes here without signing in. If you need an editable link for something already printed, the optional account gives you a place to manage it and see daily counts—also free. It is a small, practical project, not a subscription service.
