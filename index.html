WHY THIS EXISTS
Built after one too many QR-code paywalls.
I needed QR codes for my research posters, but finding a straightforward tool meant getting past ads, sign-up forms, and paid features. So I decided to build my own—and share it.
QR Studio is a practical tool, not a subscription funnel. Generation and downloads are free, with no login required. My aim is to make link management and analytics free too.
